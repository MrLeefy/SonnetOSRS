# Expansion roadmap

## Implemented in v0.2.0

- Atomic inventory/equipment/bank actions and tested actor-lifetime combat guards.
- Responsive touch presentation, reliable gestures and cancellation, real local profile selection.
- Local save validation, previous-save recovery and export/import.
- Playable gathering, cooking/smithing, contracts, PvM spawns, a telegraphed boss, XP and actual banking.
- Camera easing, quality presets, bounded effects and reusable render buffers.
- Deterministic regression tests, browser tests and build/QA automation.

## Next: improve the playable loop

- Physical Android and iOS testing, accessibility audits and Safari/Firefox support checks.
- Expanded recipes, equipment requirements, loot balancing and deeper contract chains.
- Better path planning under crowds and separate content data modules instead of more global overrides.
- Original NPC dialogue, world interactions, additional monster silhouettes and combat animation blending.
- Clearer tutorials and analytics-free performance benchmarks on real devices.

## Larger engine work — not implemented yet

- Region streaming, interiors, dungeons and a world editor.
- Server-authoritative multiplayer, accounts, anti-cheat and synchronized economy.
- PWA offline service worker, Android APK packaging, cloud saves and controller support.
- Larger content catalog, replay/spectator systems and advanced team-fighting bots.

Keep the existing renderer and style unless measurements justify a change. Preserve upstream attribution. Do not describe this offline foundation as the complete RuneScape game, an MMORPG or a measured 100× speedup.
